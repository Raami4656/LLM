[are-you-ready-guide.pdf:p15:c1] Know Local Plans and Resources Knowing what plans already exist can help you understand what you need to plan for and what resources are available. Ask local officials about your community’s emergency preparedness plans and learn about opportunities for education and training. Parents and guardians should make sure that schools and daycare centers have emergency response plans. Ask how schools will communicate with parents and guardians during a crisis, and whether they keep enough food, water, and other basic supplies
